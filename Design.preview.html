Design System Analysis of DeepSeek Harness desktop composition

The Harness product depiction puts a compact workspace/session rail beside a quiet conversation canvas. Tonal surfaces establish hierarchy, and a rounded prompt dock collects configuration and the decisive send action. These findings describe the depicted app, not the landing page's blue glow or marketing hero.

09 - Component inventory

Documented anatomy and behavior

This is the bridge back to the written design guidance. It preserves the source component names without forcing the visual sample page to copy the Markdown structure.

Workspace rail

Brand, new-session action, auxiliary destinations, grouped workspace and session rows, bottom account/status utility. Selection uses a tonal well .

Conversation

Compact title strip, user bubble, assistant document, muted thinking disclosure, and a prompt dock . Product tasks supply the content.

Prompt dock

Rounded input surface with attachment/context control, compact configuration and send control. Disabled and focus states of the live app are not observed.

Evidence pane

File/diff and trace information are adjacent secondary carriers in the source depictions. Target disclosure mechanics are an adaptation to Pi's existing views.

14 - Guardrails

Do and avoid

Short implementation constraints close the visual reference and remain visible to both human readers and agents.

Do

Compose compact navigation, readable content and an enclosed prompt together

Avoid

Copying the public page's glow, marketing hero or download controls into the app

Do

Distinguish selection and nesting with tonal steps

Avoid

Coloring every separator with the agent's terminal theme accent

Do

Keep working detail available through disclosure

Avoid

A permanently open tool/metric column dominating every empty conversation

Do

Use original Pi content, icons and interactions

Avoid

Copying source brand assets or adding unsupported automation features
